Why a waterfall

A résumé list reads as six disconnected jobs. A trace reads as one system with nested, concurrent spans — which is what a career actually is, and what this engineer works on all day.

Real dates, real numbers

Every span sits on a true month axis, Jan 2021 to Mar 2027, and overlaps are visible because they happened. The numbers hang off the iFood span as attributes — the same place a tracing UI puts them, and the same place your LinkedIn puts them. Nothing is inferred or rounded up.

The motion

Each bar grows on its own view() timeline, starting at entry 100% — the row must be fully on screen before anything moves. Bars land as each row reaches reading position, so the axis fills left to right as you descend.
